Beyond the Build

Curious by default. Hands-on by choice.

When Pillar — an AI interview companion tool — landed on her team after an acquisition, half the engineers on it didn't actually know what the product did. Arthi paired up with a senior PM and a designer to rebuild it from the inside out: tighter integration with the company's existing ATS, a long list of bugs finally closed, a workflow that actually made sense end to end. Adoption jumped soon after launch.

Off the clock, she paints, takes orders from her cats, and treats "I don't know how to do this yet" as an invitation rather than a warning. She's happiest mid-challenge, and there's always a next trip half-planned.
